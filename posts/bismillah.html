In the name of Allah: why I'm writing here

Every good thing starts with the name of Allah, so that is where this starts. This site is a place to put down what I'm learning, thinking about and building.

I don't want it to be a performance. I want it to be useful to me first, and if it benefits someone else, all the better.

وَمَا تَوْفِيقِي إِلَّا بِٱللَّهِ ۚ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَإِلَيْهِ أُنِيبُ

"My success is only by Allah. In Him I trust, and to Him I turn." (Hud 11:88)
